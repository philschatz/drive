# Tahoe trip — planning notes

We leave on **{{today+4d}}** and drive back five days later. Cabin is booked and paid;
everything below is the stuff we always forget. Notes last touched {{today}}.

## The cabin

**Pinecrest**, South Lake Tahoe. Sleeps six, so Grandma has the downstairs room when she
arrives later in the week.

- Door code is in the [rental listing](https://example.com/rentals/tahoe-pinecrest) under *Check-in*
- Parking is one car in the drive, one on the street — **not** on the grass
- No dishwasher. There is a washing machine.
- Quiet hours from ten, and the walls are thin

> The owner asks that we strip the beds and start a load of towels before we go.
> Takes ten minutes and saves the cleaning fee.

## Driving

Roughly eight hours with stops. Leave early enough to get over the pass in daylight.

1. Ana takes the first two hours, out through the flats
2. Miguel drives the middle stretch and the climb
3. Swap again at the summit rest area so Ana brings us down into the basin
4. Whoever is not driving handles snacks, music and the kids' arguments

*If the pass is closed* we go around the south side — adds about ninety minutes, so decide
before we commit at the junction.

## Packing

### Everyone

- Layers. It is genuinely cold at night even in summer.
- Swimming things, packed somewhere reachable
- Sunscreen — the mineral one, Theo reacts to the other kind
- Water bottles, one each, filled before we leave

### Maya

- Her camera and **both** batteries, plus the charger
- Book for the car
- Goggles, which live in the swim bag

### Theo

- The dinosaur, non-negotiable
- Spare shoes, because he will go into the lake wearing the first pair
- Travel sickness tablets for the winding section

### Kitchen box

We always overbuy. Bring the basics and shop once we are there.

- Coffee and the little grinder
- Olive oil, salt, pepper
- Two pans, because the cabin ones are terrible
- Rubbish bags
